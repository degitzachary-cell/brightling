Brightling

A pet made of your month.

On the App Store 24 November

Pick an animal. Each morning, tell it what yesterday was. Sleep, steps and workouts come straight from Apple Health, and you add the rest in a few taps.

Over weeks it grows into what you actually do. Good sleep shows. So does a run of late nights. It never dies, nothing resets, and there are no streaks to break.

No account. Your days and your Health data stay on your phone.
