Brightling

A pet made of your month.

Pre-order on the App Store

Out 24 November. Free on iPhone.

Pick an animal. Each morning, tell it what yesterday was. Sleep, steps and workouts come straight from Apple Health, and you add the rest in a few taps.

Over weeks it grows into what you actually do. Good sleep shows. So does a run of late nights.

Fair questions

Is it another streak app?
No. There are no streaks to break and nothing dies. Miss a week and it's still there when you get back.
What does it read?
Sleep, steps, workouts, weight and daylight from Apple Health. It stays on your phone. There's no account.
What does it cost?
Four animals are free, and the whole mirror stays free. Plus adds six more animals, habitats and costumes.
