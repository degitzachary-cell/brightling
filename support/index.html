Common questions

My sleep or steps aren't showing up.
Check Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, Brightling, and turn on the types you want it to read. If you use another tracker (Garmin, Oura, Strava and so on), make sure that app writes into Apple Health. Then open Brightling again.
How do I restore Plus on a new phone?
Open Nest, then Settings, and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
How do I cancel?
Open Nest, Settings, Manage, or go to Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Brightling. Cancel at least a day before it renews and you won't be charged again. Lifetime isn't a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel.
My subscription ended and it's asking me to pick another animal.
Plus animals need Plus. Pick a free one and everything you've logged carries over. Subscribe again later and your old animal is right where you left it.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds go through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
The weather in the app is wrong.
Brightling needs rough location while the app is open: Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Brightling, While Using. Weather refreshes when you open the app.
The widget isn't updating.
The widget updates after you open the app. If it's blank, remove it and add it again.
Is my health data private?
Yes. It stays on your phone. The privacy page has the details.
