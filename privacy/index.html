Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version. Brightling has no account and no server of its own. Your health data, your animal and everything you log stay on your phone. We never sell data, never show ads, and never see your health information.

Who we are

Brightling is made by Zachary de Git, a sole trader in Melbourne, Australia. When this policy says "we", that's who it means. You can reach us at z.degit@gmail.com.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Brightling reads sleep, steps, workouts, time in daylight, Activity rings, mindful minutes, resting heart rate and heart rate variability. It uses them to work out what yesterday was like, so the animal can reflect it.

Brightling only reads. It never writes to Health. Health data is processed on your phone and never leaves it. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, isn't stored in iCloud by Brightling, and isn't used for advertising or sold.

You can change what Brightling can read at any time in Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, Brightling.

What you type

The animal's name and anything you log in the morning are saved on your phone only.

Location and weather

If you allow it, Brightling asks for a rough location while the app is open, so the sky in the app matches the one outside. The location goes to Apple's WeatherKit service to fetch the weather, under Apple's privacy policy. We don't store it or receive it.

Purchases

Brightling Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.

To check whether Plus is active, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier made by the app, plus the App Store's record of your purchases. It doesn't receive your name, email or any health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn whether the app is working for people, Brightling may send anonymous counts of a few moments: the app being set up, a morning being logged, the Plus screen being seen, a card being shared. These go to TelemetryDeck, which turns the device identifier into a one-way code on the phone before anything is sent, and has no user accounts or profiles.

These counts never include health data, location, the animal's name or anything you type. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Sharing

If you share a card, the picture goes only where you send it, through the normal iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it saved on your phone goes with it. If you'd like the purchase record held by RevenueCat removed as well, email us and we'll do it.

Children

Brightling doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Not medical advice

Brightling is a pet, not a medical device. Nothing in it is a diagnosis or health advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Anything that collects more than this page describes would be asked for in the app first.

Questions or complaints

Email z.degit@gmail.com. If you're not happy with the answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
