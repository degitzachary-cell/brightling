Longer

Brightling is a pet that becomes what you actually do.

Pick an animal. Each morning, tell it what yesterday was. Sleep, steps and workouts come straight from Apple Health, and you add the rest in a few taps: a book, a friend, a late coffee. Over weeks the animal grows into your habits. Sleep shows. So does moving. So does a run of late nights.

Most habit apps keep score. Brightling doesn't. There are no streaks to break and nothing dies. Miss a week and it looks like you had a week off. It's a mirror, not a coach.

There are ten hand-drawn animals, from a fox and a penguin to a kitsune and a dragon, plus small things it finds along the way, from a stone on day one to a tree on morning one hundred. The sky in the app matches the weather outside.

About the maker

Zac de Git is a designer and developer in Melbourne. He made Brightling after giving up on habit trackers that felt like homework. Every animal is drawn by hand, and the whole app is the work of one person.
